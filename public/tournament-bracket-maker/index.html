Create a football tournament bracket online in a few minutes. Choose a knockout cup or a group stage with playoffs, add your teams, invite your friends and keep every result in one shared place.

How to make a tournament bracket

  1. Open UEFA Results and choose Custom League.
  2. Pick a competition: Champions League, Europa League, EURO Cup or World Cup.
  3. Choose Only Playoff for a 16-team knockout bracket, or Group + Playoff for 32 teams.
  4. Select the teams and invite your friends.
  5. Enter the results and follow the winners through the bracket, round by round.

Bracket formats

16-team knockout bracket

Round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals and final. Every tie is on one screen, so the path to the trophy is always clear.

32-team group stage with playoffs

Eight groups of four teams with full tables, followed by a knockout bracket from the round of 16 to the final.

Clubs or national teams

Run a club tournament in the Champions League or Europa League format, or a national-team tournament in the World Cup or EURO Cup format.

What you can run with it

  • A tournament with friends - one evening or a whole season, played match by match.
  • A console football cup - play on any console and keep the bracket online.
  • A competition for your office, school or club - invite the players and share one bracket.
  • All of it for free - no cost to create or manage a bracket.

Frequently asked questions

Is the tournament bracket maker free?

Yes. Creating and managing brackets on UEFA Results is completely free.

How many teams does a bracket support?

A knockout-only bracket has 16 teams. The group format has 32 teams in eight groups of four, followed by a knockout bracket.

Can my friends see the bracket?

Yes. Invite them on UEFA Results and they can follow the tables and the bracket from their own account.
