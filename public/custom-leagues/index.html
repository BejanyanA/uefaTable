How to create a custom league

  1. Open UEFA Results and choose Custom League.
  2. Pick your format - group + knockout or knockout only.
  3. Set the number of teams (for example 16 or 32) and select your clubs.
  4. Invite your friends so everyone can follow the competition.
  5. Update results and watch the standings and brackets update automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a custom football league with friends?

Open UEFA Results, choose Custom League, pick a group or knockout format, set the number of teams, invite your friends and start tracking standings together - for free.

Can I make a Champions League-style bracket?

Yes. You can build a group stage plus knockout bracket just like the Champions League, or a knockout-only tournament, and choose how many teams take part.

Is creating a custom league free?

Yes - creating and managing custom leagues on UEFA Results is completely free.
