Create Your Own Champions League Tournament

Run your own Champions League with friends - for free. Pick 32 clubs, play the group stage, go through the knockout rounds and crown your champion, with tables and a bracket that update as you enter the scores.

Create your Champions League →

How to make your own Champions League

  1. Open UEFA Results, choose Custom League and select Champions League.
  2. Choose Group + Playoff for the full format, or Only Playoff for a straight knockout cup.
  3. Select your clubs - 32 for the group format, 16 for a knockout-only cup.
  4. Invite your friends and assign each club to a player.
  5. Enter the score after each match and follow the tables and the bracket.

The format, just like the real thing

Group stage

The 32 clubs are drawn at random into eight groups, A to H, with four teams in each. Every group has its own table with matches played, wins, draws, losses, goals and points, and the top two of each group go through to the knockout rounds.

Knockout rounds

From the round of 16 to the quarter-finals, the semi-finals and the final, the bracket shows every tie and every winner on a single screen.

Why play it on UEFA Results

  • Made for friends - each club can belong to a different player, so everyone has a team to fight for.
  • No spreadsheets - enter a score and the tables are calculated for you.
  • Trophies that stay - tournament winners collect the trophy in their profile achievements.
  • Completely free - no cost to create or run your tournament.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make my own Champions League with friends?

Open UEFA Results, choose Custom League, select Champions League, pick the group or knockout format, choose your clubs and invite your friends. It takes a few minutes and it is free.

How many teams can take part?

The group format uses 32 clubs in eight groups of four, followed by a knockout bracket. The knockout-only format uses 16 clubs.

Can I use it for a video game tournament?

Yes. Play your matches on a console or on a real pitch, enter the results, and UEFA Results keeps the tables and the bracket for you.
