About UEFA Results

UEFA Results is a free football web app built as a personal project - one place to follow live scores, check standings across the biggest competitions, and run your own private leagues and brackets with friends.

Open UEFA Results →

What is UEFA Results?

UEFA Results lets football fans track live match scores across the Champions League, Europa League, EURO and World Cup. Beyond live scores, you can create your own custom group-stage or knockout competition, invite friends, choose teams, and track standings together - all for free.

What you can do

  • Live football scores – follow the Champions League, Europa League, EURO and World Cup live.
  • Fixtures & results – check upcoming matches and full-time results.
  • Standings & tables – current tables for every competition we cover.
  • Custom leagues – build your own group-stage or knockout competition with friends.
  • Invite friends – add friends, start chats, and share your competition.

How it started

UEFA Results started as a personal side project - a way to combine live score tracking with the fun of running a private football competition. It runs on modern cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers and D1) and uses end-to-end encryption for all messages between users.

Free, private and secure

Using UEFA Results is completely free. No ads, no subscription. Messages between users are end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM) - only you and the recipient can read them. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.
