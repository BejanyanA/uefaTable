Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what data UEFA Results collects, how it is stored, and how you can request its removal.

What data we collect

When you register, we store your username, email address, and a salted hash of your password - your password is never stored in plain text.

Chat messages between users are end-to-end encrypted before leaving your device. The server stores only the encrypted ciphertext - UEFA Results cannot read your messages.

We do not collect analytics, tracking pixels, or behavioural data.

How your data is stored

Account data is stored in Cloudflare D1, a serverless SQLite database hosted on Cloudflare's global network.

Your encryption private key is generated in your browser and stored in your browser's IndexedDB. It never leaves your device. Only your encrypted messages (not the key) are sent to our servers.

Cookies and local storage

UEFA Results does not use cookies. We use your browser's localStorage to keep you logged in (a secure JWT token) and to remember UI preferences such as your language and last-viewed tab. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Live football data is fetched from a third-party football data provider. Only match results and standings are retrieved - no personal user data is shared with this provider.

UEFA Results runs on Cloudflare infrastructure (Workers, Durable Objects, D1). Requests are processed through Cloudflare's global network, which may handle metadata such as IP addresses in accordance with Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To request account deletion or a copy of your data, email us at contact@uefaresults.com and we will respond within 30 days.

You can clear your local data at any time by logging out or clearing your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests: contact@uefaresults.com
