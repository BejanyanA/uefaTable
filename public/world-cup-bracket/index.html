How to create your World Cup

  1. Open UEFA Results, choose Custom League and select World Cup.
  2. Choose Group + Playoff or Only Playoff.
  3. Pick the national teams that take part.
  4. Invite your friends and give each of them a country.
  5. Enter the scores and follow the group tables and the bracket.

Two ways to play

Group stage and knockouts

32 national teams in eight groups of four, then a knockout bracket from the round of 16 to the final.

Knockout only

Short on time? Start straight from the round of 16 with 16 national teams and play it as a cup.

Not only the World Cup

The same tools work for a EURO Cup with European national teams, and for club competitions such as the Champions League and the Europa League.

Why play it on UEFA Results

  • One country per friend - everyone gets a nation to play for.
  • The whole bracket on one screen - every knockout tie and every winner, from the round of 16 to the final.
  • Group tables done for you - points and goal difference are calculated from the scores you enter.
  • Completely free - no cost to create or run your World Cup.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a World Cup bracket with friends?

Open UEFA Results, choose Custom League, select World Cup, pick the group or knockout format, choose the national teams and invite your friends. It is free.

How many national teams can I include?

The group format uses 32 national teams in eight groups of four, followed by a knockout bracket. The knockout-only format uses 16 national teams.

Can I also create a EURO tournament?

Yes. UEFA Results also has a EURO Cup format with European national teams, as well as Champions League and Europa League formats for clubs.
